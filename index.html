How it works

One car. A thousand tiny billboards.

The bodywork of a Lamborghini Aventador is divided into ~1,000 slots, each one 12 cm square. You buy the slots you want, we print your artwork on cast vinyl, and the car goes out and gets photographed. A lot.

  1. 01 · OrbitDrag the car around. Scroll to zoom, right-drag to move it across the screen.
  2. 02 · PaintClick a panel and drag. The brush dial sets how much you cover; the price updates live.
  3. 03 · PreviewDrop your logo onto the selection to see it on the metal, with the print spec the wrap shop needs.
  4. 04 · ReserveHold your slots for 15 minutes while you sort payment. Slots are sold once, first come first served.

Pricing

You pay for eyes, not paint.

Every slot's price is its area times a visibility multiplier for the panel it sits on, scaled so the whole car totals $1.1M AUD including GST. Nothing is hand-priced, so the bonnet costs more than the sill because more people will see it.

price=area×visibility×$/m²

FAQ

Questions people ask.

Is this real?

The car, the slots and the pricing model are real. This is a preview build: reservations are simulated and no payment is taken yet. The example advertisers are fictional.

How big is a slot?

About 12 × 12 cm on the surface. Curved panels are measured on the metal, so a slot on the haunch is the same area as one on the bonnet. Buy a block of slots for a bigger print.

What can I put on it?

Logos, wordmarks, campaign art. Artwork is reviewed before printing and must meet Australian advertising standards. No competitor of an existing advertiser can sit inside their patch.

How long does it stay on?

Twelve months from the day the wrap goes on. The car is driven, shown and photographed through the year, and every advertiser gets the photo set.

Why can't I select the glass or the wheels?

Windscreen coverage is not road legal and wheels are not printable. Only the painted bodywork is for sale, which is why it is white: the slots are the colour.

Can I share my selection?

Yes. The share button copies a link that opens this exact view with your slots painted in.
